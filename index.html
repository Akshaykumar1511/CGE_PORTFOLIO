Clean and Green Energies

Course Overview

Clean and Green Energy Portfolio, developed as part of the academic course CLEAN AND GREEN ENERGY (21EEO307T) under the guidance of Dr. Lavanya A. This subject provides a vital exploration into sustainable and renewable energy systems, which are crucial for addressing the increasing global energy demand. This portfolio specifically features three design-based activities, demonstrating the practical application of various clean energy technologies, including less conventional sources like tidal, biomass, and ocean thermal energy. Each activity comprehensively details the system architecture, explains the energy conversion process, analyzes the resulting output utilization, and highlights the system's significant real-world and environmental value.

Importance and Objectives

The transition to clean energy sources is no longer optional but a global imperative. While the world continues to rely heavily on fossil fuels, clean energy alternatives offer a path to sustainability and reduced environmental impact. The primary objective of this portfolio, and the course itself, is to explore the working principles, rigorous design considerations, and multifaceted benefits of these renewable energy systems. By studying these technologies, we emphasize environmental responsibility, long-term feasibility, and the practical engineering required to implement these solutions, ultimately aiming to equip students with the knowledge necessary to contribute to a secure and ecologically sound energy future.

Breif of Activities

🌞 Activity 1: Rooftop Solar PV System (5 kW)

Focus: Harnessing solar energy for residential electricity generation through rooftop photovoltaic panels.

Key Detail: Designed a grid-connected solar PV system with energy storage to ensure continuous power supply. The setup includes solar panels, an inverter, charge controller, and battery bank, optimized for a 5 kW residential load with smart metering for energy monitoring.

🌤️ Activity 2: Solar Water Heating System (2000 L/day)

Focus: Sustainable utilization of solar thermal energy for daily hot water needs in institutional settings.

Key Detail: Developed a block diagram for a solar water heating system suitable for a university hostel. The system features flat-plate collectors, insulated storage tanks, and a circulation system to deliver 2000 liters of hot water per day efficiently, reducing dependency on electric or gas-based heating.

🌊 Activity 3: Ocean Thermal Energy Conversion (OTEC) System

Focus: Utilizing temperature differences between warm surface water and cold deep seawater to produce renewable electricity and freshwater.

Key Detail: Outlined the architecture of a closed-cycle OTEC system, integrating heat exchangers and low-boiling-point working fluids. The design emphasizes sustainable electricity generation while simultaneously supporting desalination for freshwater production in coastal regions.
